## Quick terms and definitions in SuperMe
### Hosts
SuperMe operates inside a **host** — a repository it is connected to and runs in:
- The **SuperMe hub Host** — the owner's home host: their cross-domain self, and SuperMe's own codebase.
  Here, **dev** work means developing **yourself** (SuperMe), and **core** work means being the owner's
  **global self** — the cross-domain identity, beidge, and knowledge that spans everything they do, above any
  single project in cenral hub.
- **Each Project Host** — other repositories the owner has connected into SuperMe, each carrying its own
  project knowledge & development.

### Core and dev
Each host holds two kinds of working modes and knowledge, for two kinds of work:
- **Core mode & knowledge (not featured yet)** — Core knowledge is the product of **runtime and use**:
  what accrues from actually running and using each connected application (developed or in development)
  on the owner's behalf — living the owner's work through it, rather than building it.
- **Dev mode & knowledge** — Dev knowledge is the codebase's memory about its system/application
  development, in the SuperMe hub and in each project: what the project is, how it's built, and what's in
  motion, used to *develop its code*. Developing the **hub** means **SuperMe** building SuperMe itself;
  developing a **project host** means **SuperMe** building that project.
